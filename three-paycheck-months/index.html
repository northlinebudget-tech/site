Three-Paycheck Months in 2027 (Biweekly Pay)

On a biweekly schedule most months have two paydays, but two or three months a year have three. Which ones depends on your first payday of the year.

2027, Friday paydays

If your first 2027 payday isThree-paycheck monthsPaychecks in 2027
Friday, January 1January, July, December27
Friday, January 8April, October26

Paid on another weekday? Count forward 14 days at a time from your first payday; any month that gets three dates is a three-paycheck month.

How to use the third paycheck

  • Build your plan so every month runs on two paychecks.
  • Give the third one a job before it arrives: emergency fund, extra debt payment, or a sinking fund such as Christmas or car repairs.
  • Bills still follow the rule: each is paid from the last paycheck before its due date.

Quick answers

Which months have 3 paychecks in 2027?
With Friday biweekly paydays starting January 1, 2027: January, July and December. Starting January 8, 2027: April and October.
What should I do with a third paycheck?
Plan your regular months on two paychecks and use the third for savings, sinking funds or extra debt payments.
