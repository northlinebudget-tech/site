How to Budget Twice-a-Month Pay (15th and 30th)

Semi-monthly pay lands on two fixed dates, often the 15th and the last day of the month. That's 24 paychecks a year, two in every month, with no three-paycheck months.

Split the bills

  • Paycheck on the 15th: bills due from the 16th to the end of the month.
  • Paycheck on the 30th/31st: bills due from the 1st to the 15th of the next month, including rent on the 1st.

Rent usually makes the end-of-month paycheck the tight one. If the two halves are very uneven, ask lenders to move a due date, or move a fixed amount from the 15th paycheck into the next one.

Quick answers

How many paychecks is semi-monthly?
24 a year: two every month on fixed dates, such as the 15th and the last day.
Which paycheck should pay rent?
The last paycheck before rent is due, usually the one at the end of the previous month.
